How it works

A short tour of Valorem.

Valorem is a sample operator on The Rez. This prototype is the clickable design. The live app is a later phase.

Valorem, the game coin

Games that take a wager use Valorem. The word is the same in the singular and the plural. Valorem has no cash value. This prototype plays fixed sample rounds and does not decide an outcome.

Ips, the other coin

Ips reserve a numbered webinar seat and can mark a shop total down to zero. The suggested rate in the theme pack is 1 Ips per $1. Ips are not sold by themselves. A sample coin pack can include them.

Webinar seats

The map is numbered seats only. Choosing seats holds them for 45 seconds. The confirm step burns Ips in the sample wallet. Confirming extends the hold briefly. This prototype does not invent that length.

The shop

Jewelry, gold bullion, and silver bullion, with sample photographs and sample prices. A declined card completes nothing.

Free entry is a web form for signed-in players. The amount and any caps are set by the operator. See Free entry.
