Concept

What Valorem is

A sample operator for jewelry, gold and silver bullion, and live webinars. The prototype is the design source of truth: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no build step.

Phase 3 is a separate private app. This deck does not start that phase.

Theme pack

One pack, ten skins

  • Id valorem. Operator id op_valorem. Glyph ◆.
  • Field is deep green #07140e. Accent gold #d9b75f. Silver stays.
  • Cormorant Garamond and Jost.
  • Skins follow the Lumen shape: game id, display name, symbols, colors, fonts, silent sounds, copy.
  • Sponsor placements are off.

Coins

Valorem and Ips

GOLD · Valorem

The game coin. Singular and plural are both Valorem. No cash value.

SWEEP · Ips

Numbered seats and shop markdown. Suggested rate: 1 Ips per $1. Ips are not sold.

The live rate belongs to the control plane. Pack amounts of Valorem are samples.

Games

Ten tiles, no hub

  • Valorem Reels, Drop, Stack, Draw, Card, Wheel, Table. Wager games. Sample rounds are fixed. No browser outcome and no payout math.
  • Vault Stack is the Rez wager: 25 Valorem to start, 10 to mint. No boosters. No race room.
  • Valorem Span, Beat, and Siege are separate free-play tiles. Embedded builds. Nothing is paid.

Webinars

Numbered seats, 45 seconds

  1. Open a sample webinar. The map is seat numbers only.
  2. A selection holds for 45 seconds.
  3. Confirm burns Ips in the sample wallet. The extend has no invented length.

There is no live draw room, winner marquee, host console, or admin.
