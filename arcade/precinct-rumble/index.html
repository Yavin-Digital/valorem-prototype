Move
P1: WASD (arrows too when playing alone), left stick, or tap the ground (Tap-to-move)
Attack
J or Z, ATK button. Tap repeatedly for a 4-hit combo
Jump
K, X or Space, JUMP button. Attack in the air for a flying kick
Special
L or C, SPL button. Costs a little health
Dash
Double-tap left or right, or Left Shift / ; (P2: Num 0 or '). Phone: flick the stick sideways twice, quickly
Pick up / throw
Stand on a lid, cone, crate or barrel and Attack to lift it (phone: the ATK button reads LIFT). Attack again to throw, Jump to set it down
Grab
Walk into a thug. Attack to knee (third hit throws), away + Attack to throw back, Up or Down + Attack to slam him into the screen
Co-op
Desktop, up to 4 players. P2: arrows + Num 1/2/3 or , . / and Right Shift or Num Enter to join. P3/P4: press START on a controller. Join on the select screen or any time mid-game
Pause
P or Esc, START, or the II button. M toggles sound
