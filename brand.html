Voice

Sound like

  • Calm, specific, and short.
  • Name the coin: Valorem or Ips.
  • Call sample prices and hosts samples.
  • Say a hold lasts 45 seconds.

Leave out

  • Emoji.
  • Invented licenses, odds promises, or state lists.
  • Winner marquees and VIP ranks.
  • Mail-in entry instructions.

Usage

Do

  • Keep branding in the theme pack and skins.
  • Use deep green as the field, gold and silver as metal.
  • Plural of the game coin is Valorem.
  • Mark placeholders until counsel and the client replace them.

Do not

  • Put the operator name inside a game package.
  • Show sponsor placements. They are off.
  • Advertise free entry in the hero. It stays in the footer.
  • Imply this prototype pays anyone.
